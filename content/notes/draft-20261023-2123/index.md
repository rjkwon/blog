I made a [guestbook](/guestbook), like we used to have in the old days, except this one has a physical component. It's pretty inefficient and impractical. I couldn’t be happier about it. My soul has been yearning to decouple the &lt;making of a Thing&gt; from &lt;being productive&gt; and to rediscover the joy of making things that are not really necessary but just fun. And connected to the internet but with an IRL component. 

This is a meditation on
- connection
- the old web 
- translating the virtual into the physical and back again

![Photo of the actual setup, with the receipt printer and Pi connected via UART. A test receipt is seen ](first-test-print.jpg "IRL version of the setup")

![Full schematic diagram of the printer setup](guestbook-full-schematic.jpg "Life of a guestbook submission")

- I had this thermal receipt printer (Cashino)that i got with an arduino uno years ago with the intention of making some kind of clever project to print something fun 
- the green wire's connected to the ... green wire
- do things just for fun, even if they're slow and inefficient.
- submit a form on the internet --> have it manifest IRL --> post it back on the internet

![Hand-drawn schematic of my guestbook printer setup, showing the thermal printer connected to wall power on one end and to a UART adapter on the other. The UART is connected to a Raspberry Pi 4.](guestbook-printer-schematic.jpg "Schematic of the printer <> Pi setup")
